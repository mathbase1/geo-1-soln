Find the area of the shaded quadrilateral

The key is to find the exact position of the two sloping lines' intersection. Once that point is known, the shaded quadrilateral splits into two very simple triangles.

Final exact area: 503 square units
The bottom side is split into 5 + 5 and the right side is split into 5 + 5. Therefore the outside figure has width 10 and height 10.

Original picture and clean interpretation

Names used in the solution

Let A be the bottom-left corner, B the midpoint of the bottom side, C the bottom-right corner, D the midpoint of the right side, and E the top-right corner.

The two sloping segments are AD and BE. Their intersection is called P.

The shaded quadrilateral is therefore B–C–D–P.

1

Place the figure on coordinate axes

Put the bottom-left corner at the origin. One unit on the diagram represents one unit of length.

A=(0,0), B=(5,0), C=(10,0) D=(10,5), E=(10,10)

These coordinates come directly from the two 5-unit sections along the bottom and the two 5-unit sections up the right side.

2

Find the equation of the shallower line AD

Line AD passes through A(0,0) and D(10,5).

Its gradient is rise divided by run:

m = 5−0 10−0 =510 =12

Because it passes through the origin, its equation is

y=x2

Every movement of 2 units to the right along AD produces a rise of 1 unit.

3

Find the equation of the steeper line BE

Line BE passes through B(5,0) and E(10,10).

Its gradient is

m = 10−0 10−5 =105 =2

Starting at B, the point-slope form is

y−0=2(x−5) y=2x−10

Every movement of 1 unit to the right along BE produces a rise of 2 units.

4

Solve the two equations to locate P

Point P lies on both lines, so the two expressions for y must be equal:

x2 =2x−10 x=4x−20 (multiply every term by 2) 20=3x x=203

Substitute this into y=x2:

y =12 ×203 =103

Therefore

P = (203 , 103)
5

Split the shaded quadrilateral along CP

Draw the internal diagonal CP. This divides the quadrilateral into:

  • triangle BCP, with horizontal base BC;
  • triangle CDP, with vertical base CD.
BC=5,CD=5

The perpendicular height of triangle BCP is the vertical coordinate of P:

h=103

The perpendicular height of triangle CDP is the horizontal distance from P to the line x=10:

10−203 =303 −203 =103

This is why the two component triangles have the same base length and the same perpendicular height.

6

Calculate both triangle areas

Triangle BCP

Area =12 ×5 ×103 =253

Triangle CDP

Area =12 ×5 ×103 =253

Add the two non-overlapping parts:

Shaded area =253 +253 =503

Independent check: the shoelace formula

Going around the shaded quadrilateral in order gives B(5,0), C(10,0), D(10,5), P(203,103).

Area = 12 | ( 5×0 +10×5 +10×103 +203×0 ) − ( 0×10 +0×10 +5×203 +103×5 ) | =503

This is only a check. Splitting the shaded region into two triangles is the more visual method.

Final answer

The exact area of the shaded quadrilateral is

Area = 503 square units

As a decimal, 503 =1623 ≈16.67 square units.
